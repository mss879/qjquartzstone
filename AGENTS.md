## QJ homepage visual policy

The user requires a unique generated image for every visual placement. Never reuse a generated photo, slab render, sample tile, sculpture or illustration in another section or thumbnail. Commission a new image using the relevant official QJ material reference. The same asset may persist through its own continuous scroll sequence or selected-product view. The official company logo may repeat for branding. Keep diverse finishes visible and preserve authentic product codes. All generated interior scenes are concepts, not installed client projects.
