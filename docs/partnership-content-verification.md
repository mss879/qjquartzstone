# Partnership content verification

Checked on 12 October 2026 against QJ's own published About and Home pages:

- https://qjquartzstone.com/about-us-quartz-stones
- https://qjquartzstone.com/

Both pages identify QJ Quartz Stone as a collaboration between Queens Ceramic Inc. (Taiwan) and Jaysons Holdings (Pvt) Ltd (Sri Lanka). The homepage card uses these full company names and countries. Its label and caption explain the companies' relationship to QJ without implying a trade route or direction of ownership.

This confirms consistency with the company's published information; it is not an independent corporate registry check.
